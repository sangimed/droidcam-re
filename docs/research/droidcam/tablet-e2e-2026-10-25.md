# Tablet E2E acceptance — 2026-10-25

Tested POCO Pad (2405CPCFBG), Android 16/API 36, with the non-debuggable benchmark APK, hardware `c2.qti.avc.encoder`, and 1080p30 over the LAN. The code base was `f9df894` plus local changes. Build hashes and measurements are recorded in `tablet-e2e-2026-10-25.json`.

The user confirmed that the reference clock was readable and latency was low and satisfactory. No numeric glass-to-glass latency was measured.

## Functional results

- Sender suspension with TCP alive produced an RTP stall indication and recovered after resume.
- Receiver suspension induced approximately 3,950 lost RTP packets; after resume, IDR recovery restored approximately 30 fps with an empty decode queue.
- DIM stopped local preview updates while encoding continued. Restoring the preview worked.
- Closing/restarting Windows and reconnecting worked; Android released the camera on disconnect.
- Cancelling an unreachable connection did not trigger a delayed camera start.
- CSV export worked. Opening the document picker stops capture under the current foreground-only lifecycle.
- Android unit tests passed 4/4; Windows Release CTest passed 3/3.

## Observation and owner decision

The final benchmark session exported 688 samples over 689.864 seconds. Within it, the monitored interval lasted 364.946 seconds and contained 360 CSV samples. Camera and encoder rates stayed approximately 30 fps. Sender queue drops remained zero. Receiver AU drops stayed at 2 and queue drops at 6, both accumulated before this interval; no additional drops occurred during observation. Battery temperature changed from 25 to 26°C, with OS thermal state `none`.

The user requested that long-duration battery testing stop. The planned one-hour observation was cancelled after approximately six minutes without a collector failure. Long-duration battery/thermal qualification is waived, not passed; these observations do not establish battery runtime or one-hour thermal behavior.

Camera-only pause and permission-denial behavior were not conclusively tested. Private camera screenshots and device document listings are not included in this report.
