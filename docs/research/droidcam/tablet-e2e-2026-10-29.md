# Tablet E2E check — 2026-10-29

Short validation on the POCO Pad (2405CPCFBG, Android 16/API 36) connected over the trusted LAN to the Windows Release receiver. The benchmark APK was built from the current worktree and installed over USB. The tablet was on Wi-Fi `192.168.1.193`; the PC was `192.168.1.236`.

## Results

- Android `testBenchmarkUnitTest` and `assembleBenchmark` completed successfully.
- Windows Release build completed and CTest passed 3/3 (`nv12_to_bgra`, `freshness_queue`, `rtp_h264_receiver`).
- The hardware `c2.qti.avc.encoder` streamed 1080p30 and 720p30. In both profiles, Android reported approximately 30 camera fps and 30 encoder fps, an empty sender queue, and zero sender drops.
- A short 1080p30 `simpleperf` run (three 10-second samples) reported 28.4–29.2% of one CPU core, 1.07–1.13 billion instructions/s, and 3.48–3.61 billion cycles per sample. The capture surfaces both reported 1920×1080 and 30.00 fps across the measurement.
- At the end of the short run, Android reported battery temperature 22°C and thermal status `none`; CPU sensors ranged from 38.8–42.2°C. This brief sample is not a thermal or battery-life qualification.
- Windows OCR observed live 1080p30 RTP, 29 fps decode, 18 ms latest-frame age, zero decode queue depth, and no new RTP loss/reorder during the captured sample. The receiver's accumulated AU/decode drop counters were 2/6 from earlier operation.
- DIM was toggled and restored while the stream remained active. Explicit disconnect and reconnect worked. The session was left connected at 1080p30.
- The profile picker listed hardware and software encoder choices at 720p30 and 1080p30. It exposed no 60 fps profile on this tablet/build.

## Environment notes and limits

The first connection attempt failed because Private Internet Access routed the app through `tun1` (`10.9.210.128`) instead of the LAN. After disconnecting that VPN, the same LAN connection succeeded. Windows was on a Private network.

This was a short functional/performance spot check, not the grouped failure-injection suite or an endurance run. It does not validate numeric glass-to-glass latency, 60 fps, battery life, sustained thermals, camera permission denial, or the not-yet-implemented Windows virtual camera. Long battery/thermal testing remains waived per the earlier owner decision.

The JSON output from `tools/measure-android-stream.ps1` was saved locally at `%TEMP%\droidcam-live-20261029.json`; it is not committed because it contains verbose raw profiler output. No private camera screenshots or tablet file listings were collected.
